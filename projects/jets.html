Overview

Project summary

JETS is an arcade-style flight combat game that I designed and programmed independently in Unity and C#. The player selects an aircraft, learns the controls, navigates the environment, and engages enemy aircraft while managing health and score.

I owned the gameplay logic and implementation, combining programming, UI, game-state systems, and testing into a complete playable project.
