About

Creative thinking meets technical execution.

I am a creative developer and motion designer based in France, with experience in both interactive development and visual storytelling. My work combines programming, animation, and design to create engaging digital experiences.

With two years of professional Unity and C# experience in the Israeli Air Force, I developed interactive applications, simulations, and UI systems alongside a multidisciplinary team.

I have also been working with Adobe After Effects since 2019, developing skills in motion graphics, compositing, visual effects, typography animation, and video editing.

Currently studying at BRASSART Caen, I am expanding my artistic and technical skills through the 3D Animation & Immersion pathway. I am open to paid freelance, remote, and part-time opportunities in creative development, motion design, and video editing.
